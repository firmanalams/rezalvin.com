AI for Indonesian smallholder farmers · Malang, East Java

Farming guidance that fits in a chat.

Rezalvin builds AI tools that tell farmers what to do in the field, step by step, in Bahasa Indonesia, on the phone they already carry. No new habits, no manuals, no waiting for the next visit.

The problem

16.9 million farming households in Indonesia work less than half a hectare. Most of them decide alone.

16.9M

smallholder households (under 0.5 ha), up 18.5% since 2013

4.55M

smallholder farms in East Java, the most of any province. That's where we start.

29.3M

individual farm businesses nationwide

Source: BPS, Sensus Pertanian 2023

Advice arrives late

Extension workers cover many villages. By the time someone visits, a disease has already spread across the field.

SOPs are one-size-fits-all

Printed guides don't know your crop, your planting date or your weather. Farmers end up guessing or over-spraying.

Apps ask too much

Most farm apps expect new downloads, logins and dashboards. Farmers already live in chat, so that's where we meet them.

What we're building

Three tools, one goal: the right action, on time.

Working PoC

SOP Engine

Step-by-step farming procedures adjusted to each farmer's crop, growth stage and conditions. Runs on WhatsApp today, with a mobile app on the way. Built with a pesticide producer partner so the advice is grounded in real product guidance.

In development

Plant Disease Detection

An Android app for farmers and extension workers in East Java. Snap a photo of a leaf, get a likely diagnosis and what to do next, in plain Bahasa Indonesia.

[Prototype]

Field Sensors (IoT)

Low-cost sensors that send field data, such as [soil moisture, temperature], straight into the SOP Engine, so advice reacts to what's actually happening on the plot.

How it works

From a question in the field to a clear next step.

  1. 01

    Farmer asks

    A chat message, a voice note or a photo of the crop. Sensor data joins in automatically where installed.

  2. 02

    AI reads the situation

    Claude interprets the question, the photo and the field data, then matches it against the right SOP for that crop and stage.

  3. 03

    Clear steps come back

    What to do today, in simple Bahasa Indonesia. Extension workers can follow along and step in when a case needs a human.
